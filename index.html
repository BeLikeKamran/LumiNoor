Frequently asked questions

Are these lenses original and safe?

Yes — every pair is sealed and sterile with a 1-year shelf life. We don't sell loose, unbranded, or street-market lenses.

Do you offer Cash on Delivery?

Yes, COD is available nationwide. Pay when your order arrives — no advance payment required.

How long does delivery take?

Most orders are dispatched within 1–2 business days and arrive within 2–4 working days depending on your city.

How do I order?

Can I wear these every day?

Yes, they're comfort-fit lenses designed for everyday wear — just follow standard lens hygiene: clean hands, don't sleep in them unless labeled for it, and replace on schedule.
